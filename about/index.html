△ About Modern Dharma

A living
investigation.

Modern Dharma explores questions about work, purpose, relationships, creativity, responsibility and conscious living through books, journals and ongoing inquiry.

An independent publishing and inquiry studio.

Based in Luxembourg. Built around questions that remain alive long after the first answer.

△ 01 / WHY IT EXISTS

Some questions return.

Modern Dharma began with a simple observation: some questions do not disappear when they are answered.

They return. In different situations. Through different people. At different stages of life.

Modern Dharma exists to stay with those questions long enough for patterns to emerge.

  • Not a school.
  • Not a doctrine.
  • Not a source of certainty.
  • Simply an ongoing investigation.

△ 02 / WHAT WE BELIEVE

Questions that shape a life.

Many of the questions that shape a life cannot be solved once and for all.

01What is trying to emerge?
02What am I here to contribute?
03What am I still trying to prove?
04What keeps repeating?
05What is this experience trying to teach me?

The goal is not to reach a final answer. The goal is to develop a deeper relationship with the question itself.

Modern Dharma documents that process.

△ 03 / HOW WE WORK

Begin with evidence.

Most systems begin with conclusions. Modern Dharma begins with evidence.

01Experience
02Observation
03Recurrence
04Evidence
05Pattern
06Recognition

Every book, journal entry and investigation follows this same path.

Rather than telling readers what to think, Modern Dharma attempts to show what was observed, what continued to recur and what gradually became impossible to ignore.

Publishing is one expression.

Writing
Editorial development
Digital publishing

Long-form inquiry
Modern Dharma operates as an independent publishing and inquiry studio based in Luxembourg.

Books are among its most visible outputs, but publishing is not the primary purpose.

The primary purpose is investigation.

Publishing is simply one of the ways those investigations are shared.

△ 06 / THE FOUNDER

Behind the investigation.

Modern Dharma was founded by Suyash Pratap Singh.

The work explores the relationship between experience, interpretation and recurring patterns in everyday life.

Rather than offering prescriptions, the writing focuses on observation, recognition and the possibility that meaning is often discovered through participation rather than certainty.

Suyash Pratap Singh

Author · Investigator · Founder
