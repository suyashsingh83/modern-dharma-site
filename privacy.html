Privacy Policy

Overview

Modern Dharma respects your privacy. This notice explains what information may be processed when you visit this website or choose to complete the Reader Reflection form.

Information you provide

If you complete the Reader Reflection form, the information you submit is processed through Microsoft Forms and related Microsoft services. Responses are used only to understand reader experience and to support the development of the Modern Dharma project.

Website analytics

This website uses Microsoft Clarity to understand general usage patterns and improve the reading experience. Clarity may collect technical and interaction data such as device type, browser information, page visits, clicks and scrolling behaviour.

How information is used

  • To review and understand reader feedback.
  • To improve the book, website and related reader experience.
  • To understand how visitors navigate the website.

Sharing and selling

Modern Dharma does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers used to operate the website and Reader Reflection form, including Microsoft.

Your choices

You can choose not to complete the Reader Reflection form. You can also manage or block cookies through your browser settings. For more information, read the Cookie Policy.

Contact

Questions about this privacy notice can be directed through inquiry@moderndharma.life.
