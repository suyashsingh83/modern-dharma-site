Cookie Policy

What cookies are

Cookies are small files or similar technologies that websites may place on a device to support functionality, remember preferences or understand how the website is used.

How this website uses cookies

Modern Dharma uses limited analytics technology to understand how visitors interact with the website and to improve the reader experience.

Microsoft Clarity

Microsoft Clarity is used to collect general interaction and diagnostic information such as page visits, clicks, scrolling behaviour, device type and browser information. This helps identify usability issues and understand which parts of the website are useful to readers.

Advertising

Modern Dharma does not currently use advertising cookies, behavioural advertising or marketing pixels.

Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies using your browser settings. Blocking analytics cookies may reduce the information available to improve the website, but the main website content should remain accessible.

More information

For information about the handling of reader feedback and website data, read the Privacy Policy.
